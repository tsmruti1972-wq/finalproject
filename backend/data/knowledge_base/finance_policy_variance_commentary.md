# Finance Policy: Variance Commentary Standards

Material variances are reviewed at monthly close and quarterly business review.

A variance is material when absolute value exceeds 5 percent of budget or 500,000 USD, whichever is lower.

Commentary must separate volume, rate, mix, and one-time items. Do not combine recurring and nonrecurring effects.

Each explanation must include period, baseline, and whether impact is expected to continue.

Analysts must avoid forward-looking certainty language unless approved planning assumptions are referenced.
